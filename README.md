A macro photograph of a honeycomb, rendered procedurally — one ingredient,
honey, and nothing else. Irregular wax cells, some open and full of glowing
amber honey, some capped with pale wax; a thick glossy pool of honey poured
over the comb; a honey drip falling from the top edge, its droplet about to
land. Warm directional light, shallow depth of field, vignette, a whisper
of grain.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) renders the image
deterministically to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default
`/output/entry.png`): a 1024x1024 PNG, byte-identical on every run.

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
